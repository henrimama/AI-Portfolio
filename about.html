Bio
I'm a fourth-year Bachelor of Architecture student at the University of Miami, minoring in Management. I treat every project as both a design problem and a business problem: a building has to work for the people who use it and for the people who pay for it. I'm drawn to sustainable, community-scale design and am pursuing work at the intersection of architecture and real estate development.
